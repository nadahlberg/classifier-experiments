How it works

  • Every job here is a Celery task that sleeps for two to ten seconds and records its progress on a DemoJob row.
  • Scheduled jobs are queued immediately with a countdown; the worker holds them until the delay elapses.
  • A batch is a parent job fanned out into subtasks. The last subtask to finish completes the parent, and a failed or cancelled subtask propagates up to it.
  • The page polls the jobs API every two seconds — the worker never talks to the browser.
  • Jobs are scoped to your user, so everyone sees only their own list.

Periodic tasks

Beat also fires a heartbeat task every {{ heartbeat_seconds }} seconds — a CELERY_BEAT_SCHEDULE entry in settings.py. All it does is write the current time to redis.
